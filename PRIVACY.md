# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

Looper for YouTube does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

The app is a macOS Safari extension that runs locally in Safari. It reads and updates the native loop playback state on YouTube pages only to provide the loop control feature. No analytics, tracking, advertising SDKs, user accounts, or remote databases are used.

## Data Collection

Looper for YouTube does not collect:

- Personal identifiers
- Contact information
- Browsing history
- Search history
- Location data
- Usage analytics
- Crash analytics

## Local Storage

The extension may use Safari extension storage to remember loop-related preferences locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to Redbridge Company or any third party.

## Third-Party Services

Looper for YouTube is designed to work on YouTube pages, but it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google. Your use of YouTube remains subject to YouTube's own terms and privacy policy.
